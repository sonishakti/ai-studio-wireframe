The bet, and the one number that says whether it worked

We want to be the fastest platform on which someone can configure an AI agent and hear it answer, and then trust it enough to put it in front of customers.

Fast Proven Agents FPA · provisional

Agents created this week that reached a verified first answer inside 15 minutes of active builder time, and then held a real conversation of two or more turns within 14 days.

The gaming test. Strip the builder to one button that plays a canned demo and time-to-first-answer collapses to 20 seconds while nothing works. FPA falls. That is the test a north star has to pass.

Why no count target. FPA count is reported with no target: at 45% it would need ~33 qualifying new agents a week against a modelled 30–40 company-wide. The rate is the half design owns.

The first two pull against each other on purpose: making testing easier pulls in slower, less confident users, which raises the test rate and raises the clock. Read them as a pair. A speed win with a test-rate loss is a selection effect, not an improvement.

Total 180s. The 900 s ceiling is not a sum. Past this the session is a failure, not a slow success. It is the smallest natural working block a developer has (Parnin & Rugaber, ICPC 2009), not a sum. A budget is a design contract: if p75 active dwell on a row exceeds it, the row is the defect, not the user.

Two rules throughout. Kohavi's prior: most changes move key metrics not at all, and at Bing 10–20% do, so every prediction is shown raw and discounted by a third. Plan with the discounted figure. Rules the spec obeys.

  • No property may carry text a user typed. deploy_blocked emits a stable code, never the interpolated reason string, which contains campaign names.
  • Durations accumulate from visibilitychange deltas, never a tick counter, and are winsorised at 900 s.
  • trigger 'auto' is excluded from every metric: the demo agent starts a test 150 ms after mount, and left in, the headline speed number would be generated by a setTimeout.
  • Every new property key must be added to the live console's 48-key sanitiser allowlist or it is dropped silently.
  • Session replay is off by policy, so struggle is inferred from four separate scalar signals, never summed into one index.

What the pricing docs changed

Agora charges a flat $0.10 per agent-minute, and the docs say it is the same with your own key. A latency-versus-cost slider changes the Agora bill by nothing.

  • The rate card is one line: Conversational AI Engine Audio Task, $0.10 per minute, first 300 minutes free. No tier by concurrency, region, modality or model.
  • Verbatim footnote: the unit price includes the selected speech, language and voice models, and you are charged the same price even if you bring your own key.
  • Audio minutes bill separately at about $0.00099 per participant minute, so the old "$0.10/min, all in" line was falsifiable on Agora's own worked example. It is gone.
  • The managed-key list is ARES and Deepgram for speech to text, OpenAI GPT-4o-mini, GPT-4.1-mini, GPT-5-nano and GPT-5-mini for language, and MiniMax Turbo and OpenAI TTS-1 for voice. Anything else adds a second bill from that vendor directly.
  • Agora's own latency budget sums to about 1,620 ms at the minimums, and the single biggest lever is the silence-detection setting at 640 ms by default, not the model bundle. The badge says model time and names what it excludes.

What changed in the builder

The axis is now Fastest, Balanced, Most capable. Two badges: speed, which moves, and price, which is a state until a slot leaves the managed key.

  • Who owns the backend emitter (W2)?Without a name, everything server-anchored stays provisional and the north star cannot be computed as defined.
  • Fast-Proven Rate at 45 percent: accept as the target?It is 1.15 times the cross-SaaS activation mean, chosen because zero required credentials should beat the mean. Aspirational, not derived. Revisit 8 weeks after the first clean cohort.
  • Is the 20-participant moderated benchmark funded this quarter?It is the only instrument that can resolve most of the assumption ledger at this traffic.
  • Do we publish the competitive cold-start study?It converts fastest platform from an adjective into an artefact, and it may not flatter us at first.
