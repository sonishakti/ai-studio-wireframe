# P0.9 Let callers reach the agent · JTBD

Track: **v3**. Persona: **Sam, a developer**. Source: `references/v3/03-strategy/prd-v3.json` features[P0.9]. No ClickUp comments on the task, so there are no change notes. Concept A is the surface: the Deployment tab of the agent page (`tab=deploy`, P0.8) and the Go live sheet it opens for an inbound agent (`GoLiveNumberSheet`, `panel=go-live`, P0.8), plus the Numbers row that holds the number afterwards. Scope is this agent's first number and what rides on it: the number itself, the call policy, the transfer, the confirm when a number moves, and what the row says until callers arrive. Every number in the project is P3.2 and P3.3; proving people reach the agent over time is P1.8.

## Job step

If people will dial the agent, Sam gives them a number that reaches the agent, and makes sure a session ends cleanly or passes to a person when the agent should stop.

**Job statement.** When my agent is ready for callers, I want to point a number at it, say how long a session may run and when the agent may end it or hand over to a person, and then dial the number myself, so the first outside caller reaches a finished agent and nobody waits for a person.

**Why now.** Today's sheet points a number and nothing more: no call policy, no transfer, no allowlist, a warning line instead of a confirm when a number moves, no error on a failed import, and nothing after Go live says whether callers arrive (`shots/before-01-inbound-deploy-tab.png`, `before-02`, `before-03`). The v3 API now carries `call_policy` with `end_call` and `transfer` on `Number.inbound` (requirement 48), so the fields have a home; and the KPI, a first answered call within 10 minutes of Go live, has nothing to stand on while the row is silent after the save. No vendor sets a call policy on a number and only Zendesk Talk ends number setup on a real call (research gap in `more.research_brief`), so the second half is original work.

## Happy path · P0.9.a

Story: Sam wants support callers to reach the agent, so no caller waits for a person.

1. On **Deployment**, with the agent tested and the prompt written (P0.8's Readiness), Sam presses **Go live** in the Numbers row. The sheet **Go live** opens: a number in this project or a new number on the SIP trunk, then the Number select; Sam picks the spare number. Under it, two collapsed folds, **Call policy** ("Ends when the caller hangs up.") and **Transfer** ("None"). `go_live_clicked {deploymentType: inbound, hasNumber: true, testHeard: true}` (P0.8)
2. Sam opens **Call policy** and sets **Max duration** 10 min, **Max silence** 30 s, and ticks two of the four end rules: when the conversation is complete, when the caller asks to end it. The fold header carries the gray tick. `call_policy_opened {entryPoint: go_live}`
3. Sam opens **Transfer** and gives the front desk number in E.164 and one line saying when to hand over. The fold ticks. (no event; the values ride on the PATCH)
4. Sam presses **Go live**. Studio sends `PATCH /numbers/num_0110 { inbound: { agent, call_policy } }`, the header badge reads Live, the toast names the number with **Dial it**, and the Numbers row lists the number with its policy summary and the line "Not reached yet. Dial it to hear the agent." Sam dials the number from a phone, hears the agent, and the line becomes "First session today, 14:12." `phone_number_linked {source: project, entryPoint: go_live, wallMs, hasCallPolicy: true, hasTransfer: true}`, `operation_succeeded {operation: telephony_phone_number_bind}`, `channel_connected` (server), `agent_answered_production` (server, derived, G2)

Done when: `channel_connected {inbound}` for the agent and, within 10 minutes (median), the first inbound session on that number.

## Measures

- KPI: TTFDA, inbound. Active part `builder_opened` to `go_live_clicked` (same active clock, winsorised at 1800 s), median 6 min or less; number-only span `phone_number_linked.wallMs` (sheet open to success); wall clock `channel_connected {inbound}` to `agent_answered_production`, median 10 min or less. The wall-clock read is blocked until `session_ended` ships (G2); the interim read is the active part plus `phone_number_linked.wallMs`.
- Counter metric: stalled deployments (stage 9 with no stage 10 within 3 days) at 20 % or fewer (blocked on G2). The .h row line and P1.8's strip are the dial-now prompt the PRD's kill rule asks for; they ship now rather than after the 60-minute read.
- Events: `go_live_clicked`, `go_live_blocked` (P0.8), `phone_number_linked {source, entryPoint, wallMs, hasCallPolicy, hasTransfer, reassigned}` (port of 1.0.0's `source`, new `entryPoint`), `number_reassigned {fromAgentId}`, `call_policy_opened {entryPoint: go_live | number_row | advanced}`, `operation_succeeded {operation: telephony_phone_number_bind}` (in 2.1.0), `operation_failed {operation: telephony_phone_number_create, code}`, `cta_viewed {cta: dial_it}`, `external_link_opened {surface: carrier_docs | tel}`; `channel_connected` and `agent_answered_production` are server events.
- API: Ready for the number, the policy, the end rules and the transfer (`POST` and `PATCH /numbers` with `inbound.call_policy`) in the ih7axj9zx snapshot; absent in the 3.0.0 snapshot (open question 1). Missing: number purchase, lifecycle on a number, a health or reach field, `data_policy` on a number. Studio derives reach from the sessions list and records the Go live time itself.
