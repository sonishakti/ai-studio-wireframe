## Job to be done (Sam, a developer)
**Job:** Keep agents answering without a billing stop · job map stage **Monitor** (Sam watches whether it keeps working)
**Umbrella job (P1):** Sam wants to know how each agent is doing with people outside the team, and fix what fails before customers notice.
**Situation:** Sam keeps the team's agents answering without a billing stop.
**Story:** Sam's pilot is using free minutes faster than planned, and Sam wants a warning early enough to add a card before anyone meets silence.

### P1.7.a Happy path (this is what Sam does)
1. At 80 % used, the minutes banner appears on the agents list and agent pages with minutes left and Add card.
2. Sam clicks Add card; the Billing flow opens in place.
3. Sam saves the card; the banner clears.
4. Production agents keep answering past the free allowance.

### Rainy paths (each needs its own state in the prototype and a frame in Figma)
*   **P1.7.b** Minutes run out with no card: Account suspended; the banner says production agents are silent and new sessions refused, with Add card.
*   **P1.7.c** Card declined: The reason and Retry.
*   **P1.7.d** Account reactivated: Confirmation lists the runs that are paused and need Resume.
*   **P1.7.e** Minutes run out with a card on file: No banner; billing continues.
*   **P1.7.f** A run is dialing when minutes run out: The run pauses; the run and the banner both name it; Resume after reactivation (journey RK).
*   **P1.7.g** Talk to agent while suspended: The bubble names the suspension and offers Add card.
*   **P1.7.h** Sam dismisses the warning: It returns at exhaustion; dismissals are counted.
*   **P1.7.i** Minutes left cannot be read (billing outside the v3 API, G9): No guessed banner; the Billing link stays in the header.
*   **P1.7.j** Sam has no billing rights: The banner names who can add a card.

## Goal (KPI)
**In one line:** Half of accounts warned at 80% add a card before free minutes run out.
*   Metric: Card before exhaustion
*   Target: ≥ 50 % of warned accounts add a card before exhaustion. Details: ≥ 60 % of suspended accounts reactivated within 24 h.
*   How we measure: free_minutes_warning to card_added before free_minutes_exhausted, share of warned accounts. Details: account_suspended to account_reactivated within 24 h, share. Blocked until billing events ship (G9); interim: none.
*   Counter-metric: minutes_banner_dismissed, share of accounts shown the 80 % banner: ≤ 30 %
*   Assumption we are testing: An 80 % warning converts before exhaustion. Method: A/B 80 % vs 95 %; kill below 30 %.
