## Job to be done (Sam, a developer)
**Job:** Pinpoint the slow moment · job map: Locate
**Umbrella job (P2):** Sam wants to find out what happened in a session, or in a set of sessions, and why.
**Situation:** Sam finds the moment a session slowed down or broke, and what held it up.
**Story:** Sam felt a slow moment in a session and wants to see where the time went, so Sam knows what to change.

### P2.8.a Happy path: what Sam does
1. First release: the Latency tab shows a table by turn index with legs end-of-turn detection, transcription delay, LLM time to first token, TTS time to first byte and end to end; unmeasured legs read 'Not measured yet'.
2. The timeline strip shows event markers only.
3. After the turn.finished join is verified on 20 sessions: turns sit on the strip, ?turn= expands a turn in place, and its log lines show in the turn window.
4. 'Jump to first error' selects the first failing turn.
5. 'Open the setting' on a leg (from P1.6) opens that module in the builder.

### Rainy paths: when it goes wrong
*   **P2.8.b** Turn data not available: Latency table by turn index only; says per-turn timing arrives with session analytics.
*   **P2.8.c** Content is not stored: Zero retention: timing only, no utterances.
*   **P2.8.d** Over 200 turns: Virtualised strip with 'Jump to first error'.
*   **P2.8.e** An old link points to a turn that is not there: 'Turn N is not in this session'; the session opens.
*   **P2.8.f** Log lines fall outside any turn: They read 'Before turn 1' or 'After the session ended'.
*   **P2.8.g** A leg was not measured: 'Not measured yet', never dropped and never zero.
*   **P2.8.h** The session ended before the first turn: Says so and points to Status detail in the header.
*   **P2.8.i** The turn join is not verified yet: The turn strip stays hidden and the table leads until the 20-session check passes.

## Goal (KPI)
**Median time to find the slowest or first failing turn under 30 seconds.**
*   Metric: Turn found
*   Target: Median ≤ 30 s. Details: completion rate reported beside it.
*   How we measure: session_opened to the first turn_selected {isSlowest or isFirstError: true} in that session, median. Completion: share of session_opened on failed or slow sessions that reach it within 2 min. Measurable only after the 20-session join check; until then the 5-owner task is the only read.
*   Counter-metric: ≥ 3 turn_selected within 3 s ≤ 25 %
*   Assumption: Selecting a turn with its latency legs beats reading the transcript; the average Sam looks for one slow turn, not the whole session. 5-owner task; kill if fewer than 3 of 5 find it.
