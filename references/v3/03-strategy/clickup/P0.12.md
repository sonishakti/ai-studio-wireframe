## Job to be done (Sam, a developer)
**Job:** Feel sure before Go live · job map stage **Confirm** (Sam checks it is right before going on)
**Umbrella job (P0):** Sam wants to create a new agent that people outside the team can talk to.
**Situation:** Sam feels sure enough of the agent to put it in front of people outside the team. Quality row across P0, not a job step.
**Sam wants to:** trust the agent before callers hear it
**So that:** going live is a decision, not a gamble

### P0.12.a Happy path (this is what Sam does)
1. Vendor logos show on presets, Custom and voice.
2. A preset switch and the first answer each get a short motion, 150 to 250 ms.
3. Empty integrations, runs and numbers each say what goes there and offer one action.
4. After the first Go live, Sam answers one question: how confident?

### Rainy paths (each needs its own state in the prototype and a frame in Figma)
*   **P0.12.b** Sam has reduced motion turned on: Transitions are off, and the state change is still visible.
*   **P0.12.c** A logo is missing or has low contrast in dark mode: The vendor name shows as text.
*   **P0.12.d** Assets load slowly: The layout holds, with no shift.
*   **P0.12.e** Sam skips the confidence question: It is asked once and never again. A dismissal counts as no answer, not a low score.
*   **P0.12.f** Sam uses a screen reader: Logos carry the vendor name as alt text, and motion is never the only signal.

## Goal (KPI)
**In one line:** Average go live confidence rating of 4 out of 5 or higher.
*   Metric: Go live confidence
*   Target: Mean 4.0 / 5 or higher; response rate reported; no read below n = 30 per arm
*   How we measure: go_live_confidence_rated after the first channel_connected per account, mean. Response rate: rated, share of asked.
*   Counter-metric: TTFA p75 within +10 %
*   Assumption we are testing: Logos and motion raise trust at no cost to speed. Method: flag A/B with motion on vs off, surveying both arms. Kill if TTFA p75 rises by more than 10 %.
