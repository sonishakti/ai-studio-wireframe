What we take from each

  • A is the spine. One Turn-taking row above the raw rows; presets in the words that already exist; the recap says what they do; Custom points down.
  • C is the naming + the honest seats. SAL becomes "Lock onto one speaker" (same properties); Backchannels and Preemptive replies are visible, inert, captioned.
  • E is one chip. "Try interrupting" on the Test tab with a verdict line quoting the effective preset.
  • B rejected — three points are not a continuum; Custom has nowhere to stand.
  • D reduced to a recap line under Greeting. No switch (868ker6tm); the preview gate is an owner question.

Why this shape

  • The raw rows shipped last week (#1443 · #1446) and own the state. A re-presentation that reads and writes the same turn_detection / interruption is the only move that "is not duplicated" (868kytqwj).
  • "Presets resolve to visible values" (868kykhaz) — the recap is that requirement, word for word, and it survives Custom.
  • Competitors converge on words (ElevenLabs patient · normal · eager) and name controls by what the agent does; nobody leads with prefix padding.
  • Engine gaps stated on the row — "an explicit Engine dependency rather than hidden Studio work" — with the switch disabled and the tooltip idiom the list already uses.
  • Testing is ambient (v8): one chip turns 160 ms into a felt moment and gives interruption_tested a trigger.
