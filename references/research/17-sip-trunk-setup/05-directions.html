SIP Trunk Setup Directions

JTBD. When my agent already talks and the only thing left between it and my customers is the carrier my company has paid for years, I want to wire that carrier in once and hear my own phone ring, so telephony stops being the part of this project I dread.

Audited against 01-jtbd.md (24 rainy scenarios), 03-learnings.md (5 learnings), 04-before.md (15 defects) and the shipped sibling, feature 11 (sip-ladder.tsx · sip-verdict.tsx · lib/sip-trace.ts). Eight roadmap tasks, six with no acceptance criteria.

Where it landsOne door: AddPhoneNumberSheet, mounted 8 times across 6 files. One detail page: deploy/phone-numbers/[id]/number-client.tsx. It gets no third.
Agora factsprovider enum is byo | twilio: no Telnyx value. inbound_config.allowed_addresses takes CIDR and is written ["1.1.1.1"] on every number. outbound_config.prefix exists and nothing uses it. Three published Origination URIs, and Agora records which one you used nowhere.
What the market doesNobody provisions a trunk from a carrier API key. Carrier is prose at four of four and a field at none. The allowlist is an authentication mode with a stated alternative. Not one of five ends setup on a call.
The price does not move$0.10 per agent-minute, flat, the same on a BYO key. Picking Telnyx, picking a gateway or raising CPS changes nothing Agora charges. Throughput, reach and reliability are the honest axes.

Every field in this list is stored per number by the contract. The grouping is the fiction.

For.
  • It is the true topology. One credential really does carry many numbers, and LiveKit, Vapi and Retell all model it this way.
  • A rotated password is edited once, not four times, which is the strongest answer in the set to rainy 23.
  • Gives 11's fix links an address that needs no number to resolve, and gives Direct Connect a place to be a trunk type if the owner says it is one.
Against.
  • AddPhoneNumbersRequest has no trunk id and no trunk object: address, transport, credential and allowlist are all per number. A trunk list would be a grouping the API cannot store, and the first edit that touches four numbers at once is four writes that can half-fail.
  • It is a third door and a second inventory, against the standing rule, and it splits the number from the thing that carries it.
  • Rainy 11 gets worse: the per-number lock that protects a number a campaign owns has no equivalent at trunk level.
Cost. High. A new route, a new list, a new detail page, a join table, and two inventories to keep in agreement forever.

A fails the audit. Three of twenty-one, and the three it wins are the happy path: name a carrier, paste a key, place a call. Everything a trunk does after the first minute has nowhere to live.

D fails the audit too, and it is the more interesting failure. It scores ten, and every one of the ten is a state it can display. It loses R5, R6 and R23 together, and those three are one defect: a checklist completes when fields are filled, so it hands a full set of ticks to a trunk that has never carried a call. Provisioning success is not call success, and a surface whose success condition is saves cannot be made honest by adding a row.

B and C tie on the audit and separate on the contract. C is the better model of reality and the worse model of what we can store.

Verdict

B, with C's vocabulary and E's words taken as data.

A trunk is a set of rows on the number that carries it. Setup is a handover said out loud: here are Agora's three Origination URIs and the signalling IPs for your country, you paste them at your carrier, you tell us which one you used, you paste your address and credential and allowlist back, and then you call your own number and answer it. The call is the end of the flow in both branches, so Manual SIP stops printing added successfully over an untested trunk.

C's object survives as vocabulary. The word trunk is on screen, one lib module owns it, and the rows read as one thing rather than six fields. What does not survive is C's inventory, because AddPhoneNumbersRequest stores address, transport, credential and allowlist per number and has no trunk id: a trunks list would be a grouping the API cannot hold, and the first shared edit would be four writes that can half-fail. E's per-carrier wording survives as data, not as screens: CARRIERS carries each carrier's own noun and its own three steps, and the last entry is Another carrier with a name you type, so the guidance degrades and the name never does.

What we are not doing, and why

  • Not auto-provisioning. The A3 dependency was raised in July and never answered, and learning 1 answers it from the market: nobody ships it. The four setTimeout calls and the fabricated pstn.telnyx.com host come out. Quick connect keeps its name in spirit and loses the claim.
  • Not a trunks list. Contract, and the one-door rule.
  • Not a readiness checklist. Our roadmap is not a user interface, and a checklist completes on a save.
  • Not a second allowlist for media. One field exists, under inbound_config, and the SDK calls it inbound call configuration. Media ships as the line that says media travels on its own ports and must be allowed at the carrier, which is the thing the customer can actually act on.
  • Not a CPS purchase. The trunk's ceiling is what the carrier told you, typed once. Buying capacity is Billing and belongs to 22.
  • Not drawing Direct Connect. See below.

The open question, not designable without an answer

What is SIP Direct Connect to a user, and how does it differ from the Elastic SIP Trunk path? Its metering and audio-quality siblings are both delivered; the Studio row is P0 for September and nothing in references/ says what it exposes. Three answers, three different designs: a different connection type is a third branch in the sheet and a type field the contract does not have; a property of an existing trunk is one badge and one metering row; sales-led is a status we report and never a thing the user turns on. Every other open question in 00-brief.md has an honest answer that needs no Engine work. This one changes whether 17 adds a door or a label, so it ships as nothing until the owner says.

Audit corrections

Three hostile audits read the build plan attached to this verdict, on locks, on honesty and on buildability. All three refuted it. The direction survives; the plan was rewritten. This section records what the audits killed and what was kept against them, so the argument is readable and not only its result.

Killed, and rightly

  • The credential last four and the rotation age. ng-console/src/features/telephony/phone-numbers/phone-number-edit-session.ts:520 hydrates every loaded number with password: "", and src/lib/telephony/phone-number-contracts.ts:84 and :103 write the password and never read it back. No credential timestamp exists on the record. So acme_prod · ••••4f2a · rotated 8 months ago was defect 7 rebuilt with better fiction. The Credential row now shows the username and one word, Set or Not set, with a Replace password action that writes and never reads.
  • The Call capacity row, and cps with it. Nothing computes it, nothing enforces it, and the number next door disagrees: batch pace is the seed cps: { target: 3 } (lib/campaign-data.ts:1447, :1481, :1516, :1555) rendered as target 3/s at components/batch-detail.tsx:168. The brief already settled it: Requires Engine for the ceiling, and the task's own backlog note puts the control on Batch Calls. Learning 5 still holds; it is answered by the degraded batch opening its own number's trunk, not by an input.
  • The CPS fix link rewiring. With no capacity row, CPS_FAILURE keeps its one door at /deploy/batch-calls, which is where pacing is changed. Its fixSecondary, "Raise the CPS limit", is deleted rather than repointed: the ceiling is the carrier's and we cannot raise it. Only the 403 is rewired, to that number's Credential row.
  • AGORA_SIGNALLING_IPS and its Copy button. The docs list the per country ACL in accordions and this research reproduced not one address. A Copy button over addresses nobody read is the fabricated pstn.telnyx.com host moved one stage later and made pasteable. Step 2 copies the Origination URI, which is captured and real, and links the guide for the ACL. The country code row goes with the IPs, because nothing is left to index by it.
  • The carrier API key inputs. Keeping the Account SID, the secret and the scoped key toggle after deleting everything that used them is defect 3 moved from the log lines into the inputs. There is no Agora call to make with a carrier key and no field to store it. The scoped key preference survives as a principle with nothing left to apply to; the only credential in the flow is the SIP digest pair.
  • "Requires Engine · Nov". 868kubvcy is P1 and undated, and the only November in the codebase belongs to feature 07. The caption is the house default, "Requires Engine".
  • Carrier stored twice. SipTrunk.carrier beside PhoneNumber.vendor is two records for one fact, which is the defect the verdict exists to close. vendor is migrated to carrier plus carrierName on PhoneNumber, all nine seeds re-written, and the trunk object carries no carrier at all.
  • Two gateway controls. Learning 4 says gateway is never a per number dropdown. There is now one Select, in the handover step where the user is choosing which URI to paste, and a read only value on the number page.
  • Two primary fills in the number page's first fold. Save at number-client.tsx:76 stays the page's one primary. The Connection row's "Call this number" is an outline button, like Detach at :107.
  • The compile errors. The old plan left Stage tests at sip-quick-connect.tsx:142, :230 and :238-270 against a union that no longer has those members, read MOCK_NUMBERS at :216, :277 and :316 after deleting it, changed onConnected without touching its type at :61, deleted the only writers of form.sipDomain and form.username while add-phone-number-sheet.tsx:70 still required them, and deleted the Display Name field at :147-149 that canAdd also required.   • Two fabrications found next door and fixed while the files are open. lib/sip-trace.ts:229 mints Call-IDs against sbc-us-west, sbc-us-east and sbc-eu-central at agora.io; the three published hosts are the viblinx.com SBCs. Kept against an audit, and why

  • The recommended direction itself. All three audits attacked values and anchors inside B. None attacked the audit table, and none proposed A, C, D or E. B stands.
  • The selected state fix, but not onto ring-stroke. One audit prescribed components/ui/tabs.tsx:72, ring-1 ring-stroke, as the house precedent for a selected card. It is the tabs primitive and one instance. The selection card idiom in this codebase is components/wizard/radio-cards.tsx, whose own comment says it "Replaces the ad-hoc aria-pressed buttons and ToggleGroup cards that each restyled selection their own way", and border-primary with bg-primary/5 appears at eight call sites. So the carrier cards become RadioCardGroup and RadioCard outright, and the mode toggle takes the same ink border and tint the two shipped ToggleGroupItem card rows already use. Both halves match the shipped idiom rather than each other.
  • Two helper lines on a row that has an Engine dependency. One audit read the rule as one line per row always. The rule is against repeating a caption, and the house's own Engine rows already carry a description and a caption: components/wizard/engine-row.tsx:72 puts the caption in the hint slot under the description. What was cut is the repeat: Gateway location had a hint and a caption saying the same thing, and the allowlist's media line moved to the handover step where the customer can act on it.
  • Connection state. It is not a seed dressed as data: call history carries toNumber, fromNumber, callTs, callOutcome and callFailedReason, so last call got through, never called and carrier rejected are real derivations.
  • Feature 11's own event names. The plan proposed trunk_test_call_succeeded. test_call_connected already is the success line and 04-before.md lists it among the things that must survive, so no third name is minted for a status that exists.
