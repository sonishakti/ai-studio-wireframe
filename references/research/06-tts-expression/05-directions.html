What we take from each

  • E is the rule. A control appears only when the vendor has the field — but the universal ones (Pace · Tone) stay dead with the reason, not hidden.
  • A gives the one normalised handle. Pace, 50 = normal, mapped per vendor (contract in 00-brief). Expressiveness/Stability stay vendor-native.
  • C ships whole. Pronunciation on every vendor, fallback named on the row.
  • B only where it's true. Tone writes instructions/instruction on OpenAI/Generic; no system-prompt write.
  • D is one inert row with the ticket, draft in sessionStorage.

Why this shape

  • Never fakes a capability: every live control writes a field that exists in agora-agents@2.4.0; every dead one says why.
  • One normalised handle where the mapping is clean (11 vendors), vendor units where it isn't — Retell's uniform scalars without Retell's hidden remapping.
  • Fixes what the audit found on the way: ElevenLabs key + ranges, the invisible Tencent "happy" seed becomes a visible Emotion select.
  • Stays inside the v4/v6 locks: speech tuning in the slide-out, JSON door kept, flat form, one caption per control.
  • "Hear it" is honest today (library sample) and becomes real the day a synth-preview endpoint exists — same button, caption removed.
