The call

C, with B's vocabulary as its spine and E named as the next move. One module owns what a channel is and how ready it is. Two views render it: the four cards in the builder, where the journey starts, and the table in Resources, where a user reads an agent's whole reach in one place, which no competitor ships. One sheet is the door from both, and it is a sheet rather than a page because a page for a channel with no API is a page that redirects, which is the defect we are here to remove.

What we are not doing, and why

  • Not a channel matrix. The lock survives on three pieces of evidence, none of them ours. Reversing it a third time in six months needs a reason neither prior round produced.
  • Not Telegram. It is in no ticket and no contract and no release note. WhatsApp and SMS at least have Engine tickets to name. A card for Telegram would be the only thing on this screen with nothing behind it.
  • Not SMS as a working surface. It is a different runtime: turn_detection and interruption assume a duplex audio stream, and SMS is asynchronous and turn-less. It is drawn inert, in the grid, because a missing card teaches nothing and the badge is the whole promise.
  • Not a text-only agent type. We already draw one: interactionMode: "chat" renders a full typed conversation. The honest move is to mark the option we ship, not to add a second one, because channel and token are required on every pipeline join and the billed unit is an Audio Task.
  • Not the outbound half. Option B in the brief is right and it should follow a written sender identity and message-window rule, not precede one.
  • Not the retry control. It exists in no Agora contract, in no Console endpoint and in no vendor. It comes out, and the file the Console already exports takes its place as the answer.
  • Not a rename of the feature. Learning 1 shows the 2026-07-30 retirement of the word "Channel" is now factually wrong, and the row already says Inbound channels while the tab says Deployment Channels. Keeping the word costs nothing today. Changing it is a vocabulary pass across two surfaces and belongs to that pass.

The open question, not designable without an answer

Who owns the widget bundle. The snippet we print today points at https://cdn.agora.io/agent-widget.js, which 404s, and there is no embeddable widget anywhere in the Agora documentation: no page, no bundle, no script tag. Studio's own answer to "put this agent somewhere" is a copyable REST call. The SDK half of the task ships today as agora-agent-client-toolkit@2.10.0. The widget half has three possible owners and the primary action on the web surface is a different string for each: Agora hosts the bundle and the action stays Copy embed code; we ship a React component on the toolkit and the action becomes an install; or neither, and the action is the REST snippet Studio already documents. The correction ships either way, because the fake package name and the fake CDN URL come out in the first slice whatever the answer is. The button above them cannot be final until someone answers.

One thing the sheet must not become

A form. The moment it takes a WhatsApp Business account id, a sender, or a template name, it is a drawing of an API nobody has specified. It states readiness and it records an ask. Nothing else.
