What we take from each

  • A is the surface. "Opening" replaces the lone Greeting section: who speaks first → greeting → disclosure → what callers hear. Same stacked grammar, same tab.
  • B is the sentence. The disclosure is a locale default with Edit/Reset; the switch (not deletion) is the off-state.
  • D is one read-only line. The silence reminder is recapped under the opening with "Change" → Advanced. #1446 keeps the only editable home.
  • E is one button. "Hear the opening" starts the live preview, stops after the first agent turn, and prints the same "Callers hear" text as a transcript line.
  • C ships as the Advanced half. Relabel the Filler Words row; add three inert rows whose disabled switch tooltips name the Engine ticket.

Why this shape

  • Article 50 has applied since 2 Aug 2026 — disclosure is the default where the greeting is authored, not an Advanced afterthought. Recommended ON for new agents; existing greetings are never rewritten.
  • The prefix on greeting_message is the only real write. Showing the composed line is what keeps a hack honest (honesty floor).
  • "Who speaks first" mirrors Vapi/Retell/Bland and removes the Console's own trap: the preview gate on an empty greeting and the hardcoded "Thanks for calling…" default.
  • Interruptible greeting + silence recap deliver 868kuj32p's outcome ("controlled opening without trapping the session in silence") on the surface where it's read, without moving a teammate's row.
  • Engine work is named, not simulated: inert rows say "Requires Engine" and keep the builder's intent in this browser only.
