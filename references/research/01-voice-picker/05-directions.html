What we take from each

  • A is the opening. The Recommended strip + three highlighted rows at the top of the table. The full table, filters and paging stay exactly as they are.
  • B is the mechanic. A Compare column (≤3) and a tray with play/stop + Use Voice per shortlisted voice. Vendor samples in v1; "Read my greeting" disabled with its caption until the Studio preview endpoint exists.
  • D is the door. "Add your own voice" in the strip opens the id · name · consent · scope form for the active vendor. Same param path as the drawer's Custom toggle.
  • C contributes one control: ▶/■ on the Models-row Voice button for the current voice. No caption there.
  • E is not built. A disabled table is decoration on a live surface. The multi-voice contract goes in 06-proposal as the Engine ask; one ghost "Add another voice" only if the owner says yes (Q4).

Why this shape

  • The catalog has no use-case field; the agent's prompt is the use case. Reading it costs nothing and needs no API.
  • Three recommended + three to compare is the Hick's-law budget: decide among ≤3 twice, never among 62.
  • Same-script compare is the builder-facing half of 868ka247c; the benchmark can replace the tag scorer without changing the surface.
  • Cloning stays honest: an id, a name, an unchecked consent, a scope line. No fake verification, no "processing" theatre.
  • Every audio control is a real button with a visible stop state and one shared element — nothing autoplays, closing the dialog silences it.

Dependencies, stated on the surface

  • Greeting preview → "Requires Studio preview" caption on the disabled toggle. Multi-voice → not shown (868kyr1ej: update has no tts).
  • Teammate branch codex/add-voice-clone lands first; our form must read as Custom in the drawer's inferred mode.
