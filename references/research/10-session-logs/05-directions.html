What we take from each

  • A is the spine. One clock in the existing sheet: player state lifted up, lines carry the time, click seeks, turn map on the scrubber via BipolarWaveform. Untimed calls get one line and a dead time column.
  • B becomes badges on the line. end.type words (Interrupted · Ignored · Error, plus Greeting) on the agent line; caused_by and per-turn ms in the title. The Latency tab already is the ladder.
  • E keeps its Download menu. Recording via the orphan endpoint, transcript as .txt/.json. Save-as-test moves to feature 15 on the same line model.
  • D shrinks to Copy link. A search-param deep link (?tab=call-history&call=…) gives P2 the pasteable URL now; the routed page waits for the owner's call on tension #2.
  • C folds away. Payloads, when the Engine ships them, go inline per turn as a collapsed disclosure (Wave 1, Retell, ElevenLabs) — not a tab. Nothing renders until then.

Why this shape

  • Only A works on today's fields and stays honest when the alignment fields are absent — which is every managed-vendor call until Engine lifts the llm.vendor='custom' gate.
  • The sheet is the frozen surface; everything here is additive inside the Transcript tab and the header. No lock touched, no re-split.
  • One clock is what 868kbyqfx asks for verbatim and what every competitor keys the transcript on.
  • Badges and export attach to the same line model with no new chrome; a second tab or page repeats the Wave 1 "five tabs" mistake.
  • A new route reopens tension #2; Copy link does the job without deciding the IA for the owner.
