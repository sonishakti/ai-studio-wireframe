X1 — Free-tier truth & spend cap

The Billing “Usage & spend” card: an honest 300-minute free-tier meter, an estimate-labeled projected bill, and a spend cap the user owns — the guardrail that makes the “drive volume” activation strategy safe under postpaid billing.

● Shipped 2026-07-09 commit 24ad614 Live: /billing ↗ PRD: roadmap-features-prd-2026-07-09.md §3 · X1

Why this feature exists

Agora earns revenue only when minutes flow on a live deployment — so the whole activation strategy pushes users toward volume. But ConvoAI billing is postpaid ($0.10/min after 300 free min/month), and volume without a visible ceiling produces surprise invoices. Bill-shock is the churn engine that would eat every conversion the funnel wins. X1 is the counter-metric made into UI: the free tier shown truthfully, the projected bill always visible and always labeled an estimate, and a cap that pauses new calls before money gets scary.

Land→ Believe→ Connect→ Consume→ Convert · X1 guards this→ Retain
The fact-check that shaped it: ConvoAI’s free tier is 300 min/month (shared with Real-Time STT + Translation) — not the 10,000 minutes some surfaces implied. 10k belongs to core RTC voice. The split 150 (no card) + 150 (card unlocks) is the locked 2026-06-22 nudge design; X1 makes that split legible in the meter itself without moving the nudge moment.

Diverge → converge

Three variants were built on a throwaway harness (/billing/spend-lab, commits a570f62…3342b3e, deleted after judging per the /agents/proto convention) and scored by three independent judge lenses.

A · Consolidated card

One card stacking meter → projection → inline cap/alert form. Best meter a11y and cap-hit prose; lost on an unclamped projection above a “never exceeds” promise, banners buried at the bottom, and an inert raise-cap loop.

B · Meter band + controls row

OpenAI-Limits read/write split. Only variant whose money story survived every scenario: projection always clamped to the cap, every recovery loop rendering its consequence.

C · Compact card + setup sheet

Calm glance card; considered 3-beat cap sheet. Best IA fit and the best at-the-cap education; lost on a broken resume loop and a chore-CTA at the capture moment.

What shipped

Anatomy — usage-spend-card.tsx

  1. State banner (leads the card): threshold · paused · cap-warning · cap-hit · transient confirmations
  2. Hero: minutes while free (“150 of 300 free minutes used”) → projected bill once PAYG (“$40.74 · Estimate”)
  3. Meter: split-tier 150+150 (locked half = expandable capacity) → dollars-of-cap with alert tick
  4. Quiet controls row: cap chip + “Edit spend controls” → 3-beat sheet
  5. Disclosures: metering lag + cap semantics in one calm line

Design decisions that bind

  • Projection on display never exceeds the cap; the clamp is disclosed (“run rate alone would be $62 — your $50 cap holds the invoice”)
  • Cap is settable before a card exists; it arms at capture
  • “Keep paused” is a first-class choice, not a dark-pattern dead end
  • One card-capture flow product-wide (AddCardSheet reused from the Monitor nudge)
  • Every figure derives from PLAN_USAGE via freeMinutesStats()/spendStats() — surfaces can’t disagree

Data & events

PlanUsage gained spendCapUsd · spendAlertPct · paygSpendUsd · periodLabel · periodDaysElapsed/Total; spendStats() mirrors freeMinutesStats() (state machine lives in the lib, never re-implemented in components). New events: spend_cap_set · spend_cap_raised · spend_cap_hit · spend_alert_fired · projected_bill_viewed.

Counter-metric: spend_alert_fired must precede spend_cap_hit for the same cap. A cap hit with no prior alert is the failure this whole surface exists to prevent — dashboard it that way.

Also fixed in this pass

  • /billing/usage quota row was hardcoded “Conversational AI 18,420 / 50,000 min”, contradicting the 300-min truth — now derives from freeMinutesStats(); the 10,000-min row is relabeled “Voice minutes (RTC)” because that allowance belongs to core RTC, a different product.
  • The Billing period label was hardcoded “May 1 – May 31” — now reads PLAN_USAGE.periodLabel.

Open follow-ups

  • A6 hook: the cap sheet is where a concurrency-purchase upsell could co-live — decide when A6 (self-serve concurrency) is designed, not before.
  • Alert delivery: the 75% alert fires a bell/email in copy only (mock); D6 Alerting will own real delivery.
  • Reconcile with Aria’s Monitor nudge cadence if PLAN_USAGE personas ever diverge per surface.
