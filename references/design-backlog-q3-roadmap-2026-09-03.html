Research plan

Eight questions, ordered by how many P0 items each unblocks and how soon they are due. Methods reuse what is already in references/prompt-library.md and the user-test workflow; nothing here needs a new tool.

Method
Unblocks
R1
Which agent-behaviour controls do developers actually reach for, and in what order?Twelve new P0/P1 builder controls are queued. The builder is already at five collapsible sections; adding twelve rows without a hierarchy breaks the Test Strip layout.
Card sort of the 22 control tasks with 6–8 developers (/organize), then a competitor teardown of Vapi, Retell and ElevenLabs builder panels (competitor-teardown prompt). Output: control taxonomy + "advanced" fold rule.
Agent builder (28 items) · shared behaviour-control contract
R2
What does a developer need to see to trust a phone number they bought from Agora?Phone Number Resell is XXL and due September. It reverses the BYO-SIP lock and touches billing, compliance and the first-run home.
Journey map of buy → verify → assign → first inbound call (/journey), with a Twilio / Telnyx / Vapi purchase-flow teardown. Pair with the Bandwidth PoC owner for the compliance steps that cannot be skipped.
Telephony (15 items) · Deploy first-run · billing APIs
R3
When a live call goes wrong, what does the operator need in the first ten seconds?Live monitoring, sentiment, nudges and take-over are all P1/P2 and all previously locked out. One decision unblocks five items.
Think-aloud with three operator personas on a mocked live-call panel (user-test.js, focus = intervention), preceded by a stakeholder interview on what verbs are permitted (/investigate).
Monitor live view (5 items) · live-call overseer
R4
How do developers evaluate an agent before and after a change?Evals, scorecards, production A/B and simulations are seven separate tasks describing one loop.
Diary-style interviews with 4 developers who ship agents weekly, mapping their current eval ritual (/investigate); then a blueprint of the build → test → ship → score loop (/blueprint).
Test & evals (13 items) · versioning · A/B placement
R5
What does "usage" mean to a developer paying per minute across managed and BYO vendors?Model-level breakdowns, credits, concurrency self-serve and Archer all land on the Usage page in September.
Heuristic evaluation of the current Usage and Billing pages (/evaluate) against the Q3 asks, then a pricing-comprehension test with 5 developers using real numbers from the verified pricing notes.
Usage · Billing · Managed (8 items)
R6
What should a Console-and-Studio login actually unify?P0, September, and it is the seam this whole project exists for.
Stakeholder interview with the PM who filed it, then a 5-step cognitive walkthrough of signup → first agent across both apps (/evaluate). Cheap, and it decides whether Studio_X's premise is the roadmap's premise.
Platform & governance (9 items) · funnel north star
R7
Where does a developer expect a handoff to be configured: on the agent, the deployment, or a canvas?AI triage, warm transfers and the workflow canvas are P0/P1 and depend on the agent-vs-deployment split.
Tree test of the current IA with the handoff tasks inserted (/organize), plus a Retell / Bland / Vapi routing teardown.
Workflows & routing (7 items)
R8
Do developers want to choose a voice, or be told which one?"Compare and recommend voices" is P0 for September; voice cloning is P0.
Preference test with 8 developers: gallery vs recommendation vs use-case wizard, using real voice samples from the vendor catalog. Fold cloning consent and governance into the same session.
Voices & vendors (10 items)

Next two sprints (research)

Assumes one designer plus Claude, two-week sprints, planned to about 70 percent. Team, PTO and the real sprint dates are yours to fill in; the sequence is what matters.

Sprint A — September due dates

Goal: every P0 due in September has a research read-out before it is drawn.
  • R6 unify login — stakeholder interview + walkthrough (2 days)
  • R2 phone number purchase journey (4 days)
  • R5 usage and pricing comprehension (3 days)
  • Owner calls on the six lock conflicts (half day, one meeting)
  • Stretch: R8 voice preference test

Sprint B — the builder and the loop

Goal: a control taxonomy and an eval loop the builder can grow into without breaking the Test Strip.
  • R1 control card sort + teardown (4 days)
  • R4 eval loop interviews + blueprint (4 days)
  • R3 live-call intervention test (2 days, only if the owner call permits operator controls)
  • Stretch: R7 handoff tree test
